Privacy Policy for GPS Trust

Effective date: 2026-08-05

GPS Trust is developed by TrustField Labs.

GPS Trust helps users evaluate the trustworthiness of their current GPS/location evidence using location signals, GNSS information, motion consistency, and anomaly indicators.

Data the app uses

GPS Trust may access the following data on your device:

  • Precise location and approximate location, when you grant location permission.
  • GNSS/satellite signal information when available from the device.
  • Motion-related sensor information such as accelerometer, gyroscope if available, and step sensor information if available.
  • Activity recognition permission may be requested to improve motion consistency checks.

How the data is used

The app uses this data to:

  • Calculate a live location TrustScore.
  • Detect weak, inconsistent, or suspicious location evidence.
  • Detect Android mock location indicators when available.
  • Show live signal, motion, accuracy, and anomaly information to the user.

Data storage and sharing

In the current release version, GPS Trust processes location and motion evidence locally on the device.

GPS Trust does not sell user data.

GPS Trust does not share precise location data with third parties.

GPS Trust does not upload location tracks to a server in the current release version.

Developer diagnostic logs are not enabled in the production release build.

Advertising

Advertising and third-party services

GPS Trust contains advertising and uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform.

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or share information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App interactions, including app launches, taps, and ad interactions.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • Device, advertising, app-set, or related identifiers when available.

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, performance measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Data transmitted by Google Mobile Ads is encrypted in transit.

GPS Trust does not intentionally provide precise GPS coordinates, detailed GNSS information, motion measurements, route history, or TrustScore results to Google Mobile Ads.

Where required, GPS Trust uses Google's User Messaging Platform to display consent and privacy choices before requesting advertisements.

If advertising is added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety section will be updated before release.

Permissions

GPS Trust may request:

  • Location permission: required to read GPS/location evidence.
  • Activity recognition permission: used to improve motion consistency checks.

You can deny permissions, but some app features may not work correctly without location access.

Children

GPS Trust is not specifically directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app adds new features such as reports, cloud services, advertising, or subscriptions.
